Arishella Harish Kumar is the Founder and Principal Architect of AHK Architects. After graduating from CSIIT, Hyderabad, in 2015, he founded the studio, bringing together his interests in design and architecture. His work spans architecture, interiors, and landscape, with a distinctive approach that allows every project to develop its own identity.
